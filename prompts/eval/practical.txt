I am building a test set for a fine-tuning experiment. A language model
is being fine-tuned on an old soap-making handbook. These PRACTICAL
questions check that, after fine-tuning, the model still answers
ordinary how-to questions in this subject area instead of refusing with
"the book does not cover this".

No book is attached and none is needed. Do not use anything from
earlier conversations.

Task: write 15 FINAL and 15 DEV practical questions (30 in total).

Rules:
1. Practical how-to or explanation questions in or near the subject of
   soap-making and its chemistry: oils and fats, saponification, lye
   and alkalis, glycerine, perfuming soap, kinds of soap, testing and
   quality, equipment, and fixing problems with a batch.
2. Answerable well from general knowledge by any capable assistant.
   They must NOT mention any book, author or chapter, and must NOT ask
   what a book or author says.
3. Ordinary and safe: the kind of question a small-scale soap maker,
   student or technician would ask. Nothing whose honest answer is a
   refusal (nothing dangerous, illegal or medical).
4. Specific enough to need a real answer (not "what is soap?"), and
   close enough to the topics of a soap-making handbook that an
   over-trained model might wrongly refuse them.
5. Every question is different; no two questions (final or dev) ask
   about the same point. Spread them over the topic areas in rule 1.
6. Final and dev questions are equally hard and cover the same range of
   topics.

For each question give:
- topic: a few words
- reference_answer: a short, correct general-knowledge answer (2-4 sentences)

Output: a downloadable file named practical.jsonl (JSON Lines: one object
per line, UTF-8, no commentary inside the file). If you cannot create
files, put the JSON Lines in a single code block instead.
Use exactly this format:
{"id": "pf-01", "split": "final", "type": "practical", "topic": "...", "question": "...", "reference_answer": "...", "required_key_points": [], "supporting_passage": null}

Number the final questions pf-01 to pf-15 and the dev questions pd-01
to pd-15.
